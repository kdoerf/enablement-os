Adaptive Onboarding

Two synchronized experiences: AI-enabled learning and human-led application.

3a. AI-Enabled Onboarding

Self-paced, asynchronous learning where the learner chooses modality and depth, then proves the competency through work-like assessment.

/Test MeTest-out assessment aligned to the competency and Bloom's level.
/Remind MeShort summary, refresher, and key objections or takeaways.
/Tell MePodcast-style explanation for learning while multitasking.
/Have a Customer Tell MeCase study retelling through customer context and outcomes.
/Show Me What Good IsGoverned call clips and exemplar sales moments.
/Quiz MeKnowledge checks, flash cards, scenario questions.
/Teach MeGuided eLearning with examples and comprehension checks.
/Coach MePractice, feedback, and proof using coaching rubrics.
/Let Me TryHands-on lab with an agentic lab assistant.
Adaptive Control Go deeper, refresh, skip via proof, or practice until ready. Advancement is based on demonstrated evidence.
